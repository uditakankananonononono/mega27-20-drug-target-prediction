1. Three Biggest Weaknesses (ISEF Judge Perspective)

Massive Unexplained Performance Gap vs. Baselines Under Identical Metrics
Achieving a test concordance index (CI) of 0.7533 (best) compared to DeepDTA’s published 0.878 is a severe deficit. Attributing this primarily to compute limits (single-pass chunks) or smaller encoders is insufficient for a top-tier ISEF entry. Without proving that your architecture achieves parity with DeepDTA when given identical training budget and full-epoch gradient passes, a judge will assume the lower performance stems from structural model bugs or improper hyperparameter tuning.

Incomplete Methodological Rigor & Protocol Violations
Training on arbitrary 8k/16k subsets per chunk instead of standard full-dataset epochs introduces severe epoch-to-epoch variance and sampling noise. Furthermore, switching loss functions mid-stream without maintaining strict hyperparameter controls weakens the core ablation studies. For an ISEF project, non-standard training mechanics must be strictly ablated against standard full-batch Adam training to prove they do not penalize model convergence.

Inadequate Benchmark Splitting Discipline
Evaluating on a standard random split of DAVIS allows the model to memorize shared kinase/compound families across train and test sets. To prove real-world generalization, the benchmark must evaluate on strict cold-target (unseen kinase sequences), cold-drug (unseen SMILES), or double-cold splits. Achieving high CI on a random split while failing to reach established baselines leaves the model's true out-of-distribution generalization completely unverified.

2. The Single Strongest Concrete Improvement for R2

The Improvement: Replace the 1D protein sequence CNN with a Pretrained Protein Language Model (pLM) (e.g., ESM-2) to extract frozen residue-level embeddings, paired with a deeper, multi-head GATv2 (Graph Attention Network) for the drug graph.

Why it works: DeepDTA relies on simple 1D CNNs, which fail to capture long-range structural dependencies, 3D binding pocket topology, or subtle sequence mutations. ESM-2 embeddings encode evolutionary, functional, and structural context across the entire protein sequence, directly addressing the feature representation bottleneck.

Evidence Required to Prove Success:

CI / MSE Parity: Reaching a held-out test CI≥0.878 and MSE≤0.261 under the exact standard 5-fold cross-validation protocol used by DeepDTA.

3. The Single Most Novel Contribution

Contribution: An Automated, Open-Source Data-Integrity Audit & Correction Pipeline for DTA Benchmarks (The DAVIS Audit Tool).

Most high school DTA projects blindly train models on public benchmarks without inspecting underlying data quality. Uncovering that all 54 mutant kinase entries in the widely used DeepDTA distribution of DAVIS carry byte-identical sequence representations (erasing all mutation signal while leaving distinct pKd labels) is a high-value finding. Expanding this into a comprehensive benchmark audit suite—detecting sequence truncation errors, label leakage, and missing variant features across DAVIS, KIBA, and BindingDB—gives the project immediate utility for the broader computational biology community.
