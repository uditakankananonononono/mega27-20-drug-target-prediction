# Frozen plan H4: kinase-family-held-out DAVIS diagnostic evaluation study. Written before any H4 computation.
Framing: DIAGNOSTIC EVALUATION, not a novel method. Cold-protein and sequence-disjoint DAVIS evaluation is prior art (DAVIS-complete, GASI-DTA S2, DeepDTA baselines); no novelty or benchmark claim. Main's screen (bounded, not exhaustive) found no exact match for this split + comparator pair, which is not a guarantee. R3 unchanged. Scope: this lane's pinned DAVIS mirror; label array still unverified against historical labels.
Data: pinned Y (pKd = -log10(Kd/1e9)), ligands, proteins, KinHub table (sha256 in data/davis_pin).
Family mapping: base gene = protein name with (mutation) text removed, trailing -phosphorylated/-nonphosphorylated/-autoinhibited suffixes removed; matched case-insensitively to KinHub xName, Manning name or HGNC name. Group unit = KinHub Family (column 6). Exploratory count before freeze: 381 of 442 entries map to 89 families; 61 entries (PIK3*, PIP5K*, MEK*, p38-*, aliases) do not map. Unmapped entries are NOT given families by hand: they always stay in training and are never scored.
Folds: 5 folds, families (mapped only) assigned to folds by seeded greedy balancing on row count (seed 0, largest family first to the lightest fold). Mutants and phospho entries share a family with their WT, so they are held out together. For fold k: test = all rows of fold-k families; train = all other rows including unmapped entries. No fold file selection after seeing results.
Leakage-safe unseen-protein rule (set before running): held-out proteins get NO protein effect estimated from any test label. All statistics (global mean, ligand means, protein effects, similarity neighbors) are computed from train rows only. For a held-out protein the additive protein effect is 0 (centered), so the additive model equals the ligand-only model for every scored row. This is stated up front: in this study the "additive" comparator IS the train ligand-mean model. It is not trivial (ligand-only explained about 20% of variance on setting 1: MSE 0.645 vs 0.802), so H4 proceeds. If ligand-only MSE under family hold-out is within 0.02 of the global-mean MSE, state that the comparator is near-trivial and that the study is then only a test of whether sequence similarity adds anything.
Models (one frozen configuration each, no tuning): M0 train global mean. M1 train ligand mean (a ligand missing from train falls back to M0, expected never). M2 sequence-kNN: pred(l,p) = mean of y(l,p') over the 5 train proteins p' with highest cosine similarity of 3-mer count vectors to p, among proteins that have a train label for ligand l; falls back to M1 if none. M2 is the only interaction-aware model.
Metrics: pooled MSE over all scored rows across 5 folds, per-fold MSE, Harrell CI on pooled scores, and bootstrap (2000 seeded resamples of proteins, seed 0) 95% CI for MSE(M2)-MSE(M1). Reference rows from H3 (not recomputed): setting-1 additive MSE 0.558, ligand-only 0.645.
Reporting: every number as measured. Claim allowed: only the measured MSE gap between M1, M2 and the random-fold reference under this split. No claim that family hold-out is harder in general, no claim vs published DAVIS numbers.
Stop rule: one run. No re-seeding, no alternative family column, no relabeling of unmapped entries after seeing results.
